# E-commerce Sales & Customer Retention Analysis

## Business Problem
The goal of this project was to analyze e-commerce sales performance and customer behavior using transactional data.
The analysis focuses on revenue trends, customer retention, and revenue concentration to identify key business insights.

## Dataset
The project is based on the Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset (Olist), which contains transactional data including:

-Customers

-Orders

*Order items

*Payments

*Products

The dataset covers the period from September 2016 to October 2018

## Tools Used
MySQL (data analysis & querying)
DBeaver (database management)
Microsoft Excel (dashboard & visualization)
GitHub (project documentation)

## Key KPIs
The following business metrics were calculated:
Total Revenue
Total Orders
Total Customers
Average Order Value (AOV)
Repeat Customer Rate
Monthly Revenue Trend
AOV was calculated at the order level due to the presence of multiple payments per order.

## Key Insights
Only 3.12% of customers made more than one purchase.
One-time customers generated 94.10% of total revenue.
Revenue concentration analysis showed that the top 10% of customers generated 38.51% of total revenue.
The business heavily depends on new customer acquisition.

## Revenue Concentration Analysis
To evaluate revenue distribution across customers, a Pareto-style analysis was conducted.
Results indicate that revenue is relatively distributed across the customer base and does not follow the classic 80/20 rule.
